Formulas & Design Rules
Every calculation used in solar system design
🔋 Battery
Energy
Battery Total Energy
Total energy stored in the battery bank
Battery Energy (Wh) = Voltage (V) × Capacity (Ah) Example: 24V × 200Ah = 4,800 Wh = 4.8 kWh
Usable
Usable Battery Energy (DoD)
Energy safely available based on battery type
Usable Energy (Wh) = Voltage × Capacity × DoD Depth of Discharge (DoD) by type: • LiFePO₄ = 90%  • Lithium NMC = 80% • AGM / Gel / Flooded Lead-Acid = 50% Example: 24V × 200Ah × 0.90 = 4,320 Wh usable
Discharge
Battery Discharge Current
Current drawn from battery by the inverter
Discharge Current (A) = Load Watts ÷ Battery Voltage Example: 2000W ÷ 24V = 83.3A Safe discharge limits: • LiFePO₄ / NMC ≤ 1C (1× Ah rating) • Lead-Acid ≤ 0.2C – 0.3C (20–30% of Ah)
Charge
Battery Charge Rate
Current entering battery from solar
Charge Current (A) = Solar Panel Watts ÷ Battery Voltage Example: 800W ÷ 24V = 33.3A Safe charge rate limits: • LiFePO₄ / NMC ≤ 0.5C • Lead-Acid ≤ 0.1C – 0.2C
☀️ Solar Panels
Production
Daily Solar Production
Expected energy generated per day
Daily Production (Wh) = Panel Watts × Peak Sun Hours Example: 800W × 5h = 4,000 Wh/day Peak Sun Hours (PSH) = hours of equivalent full-sun irradiance. Tropical areas ≈ 4–6h, temperate ≈ 3–5h.
SCC
Solar Charge Current
Current flowing from panels into the SCC
Solar Current (A) = Panel Watts ÷ Battery Voltage Example: 800W ÷ 24V = 33.3A → need ≥40A SCC
MPPT
MPPT Oversizing Rule (130%)
Safe maximum panel power for an MPPT controller
Max Panel Watts = SCC Current × Battery Voltage × 1.3 Example: 60A × 24V × 1.3 = 1,872W max panels MPPT can safely handle 20–30% panel oversizing because clipping only occurs at peak irradiance.
🔌 Inverter
Current
Inverter DC Draw
Current pulled from battery by inverter at full load
Inverter Current (A) = Inverter Watts ÷ (Battery V × Efficiency) Example: 2000W ÷ (24V × 0.90) = 92.6A Always use efficiency (0.85–0.95). Default: 0.90
Surge
Inverter Surge Current
Peak current during motor/compressor startup
Surge Current (A) = Surge Watts ÷ Battery Voltage Example: 4000W ÷ 24V = 166.7A surge BMS rating must exceed surge current. If BMS < surge current → risk of BMS tripping or damage.
⏱ Runtime & Autonomy
Runtime
Battery Runtime
How long battery lasts under a given load
Runtime (hrs) = Usable Energy (Wh) ÷ Load Power (W) Example: 4320Wh ÷ 500W = 8.6 hours
Autonomy
Days of Autonomy
Days battery can run without solar recharge
Days of Autonomy = Usable Energy (Wh) ÷ Daily Load (Wh/day) Example: 4320Wh ÷ 2160Wh/day = 2 days autonomy
Charge
Battery Charge Time
Time to fully charge a depleted battery
Charge Time (hrs) = Battery Total Energy (Wh) ÷ Solar Power (W) Example: 4800Wh ÷ 800W = 6 hours Actual time is longer due to CC/CV charging stages and system losses (~80% efficiency factor).
🔧 Wire Sizing & Voltage Drop
Design
Design Current (Safety Factor)
Always size wire 25% above operating current
Design Current (A) = Operating Current × 1.25 Example: 33.3A × 1.25 = 41.6A → use 6 AWG (60A rated)
V-Drop
Voltage Drop Calculation
Voltage lost across wire run (use 2× length for return)
V-Drop (V) = (2 × Length × Current × Resistance) ÷ 1000 Resistance = wire resistance in mΩ/m (see AWG table below) V-Drop % = (V-Drop ÷ System Voltage) × 100 Limits: Solar wires ≤ 3%  |  Battery/Inverter ≤ 2%

🔴 Breakers & Fuses
Breaker
Breaker / Fuse Sizing
Protection rating for each circuit
Breaker Size (A) = Circuit Current × 1.25 Round up to nearest standard size (10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 40, 50, 60, 80, 100A…) Example: 33.3A × 1.25 = 41.6A → use 50A breaker
Panel → SCC breakerSolar Current × 1.25
SCC → Battery breakerSCC Rating × 1.25
Battery → Inverter breakerInverter Current × 1.25
🛡 BMS Sizing
BMS
Required BMS Rating
BMS must handle inverter load plus safety margin
Min BMS (A) = Inverter Current × 1.25 Example: 92.6A × 1.25 = 115.7A → use 120A or 150A BMS If surge current > BMS rating → BMS trips under load. Always check surge!
📋 Required Component Sizing
SCC
Minimum SCC Size
Min SCC (A) = Solar Panel Watts ÷ Battery Voltage × 1.25 Example: 800W ÷ 24V × 1.25 = 41.7A → use 50A SCC
Panels
Required Panel Power
Minimum panels to cover daily load
Min Panel (W) = Daily Load (Wh) ÷ Peak Sun Hours Example: 3000Wh ÷ 5h = 600W minimum panels Add 20–30% buffer for real-world losses and cloudy days.
Battery
Required Battery Capacity
Minimum Ah for target autonomy days
Min Battery (Ah) = (Daily Load × Autonomy Days) ÷ (Voltage × DoD) Example: (3000Wh × 2 days) ÷ (24V × 0.90) = 277.8Ah → use 300Ah
💡 Pro Tip Always oversize components by 20–25% for future expansion and to reduce stress on components — it extends their lifespan significantly.
